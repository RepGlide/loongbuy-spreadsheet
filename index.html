Do Not Zoom Beyond the Evidence

A Wider Label Is Not a Specific Fact

Overview and category labels set the frame. They cannot stand in for the specific options and facts on the current page.

1What a wider level can tell you

  • A direction and an entry point.
  • A narrower product range.
  • The visible clues at hand.
  • A place to begin re-checking.

2What a wider level cannot tell you

  • A specific option or variant.
  • A current price or stock state.
  • What the current page shows now.
  • A confirmed, current fact.
Detail boundary

A wider label is not a substitute for the current page’s specific options and facts. When the evidence runs out, stop — do not zoom past it.

Stop When the Detail Disappears

When the Page Runs Out of Detail

A missing detail is a boundary, not a reason to keep guessing.

  • 1

    Stop at the boundary. When the current page stops showing a detail, do not borrow it from a wider level.

  • 2

    Re-read the current page. Older descriptions and list thumbnails do not stand in for what is shown now.

  • 3

    Do not zoom past the evidence. A wider label cannot prove a specific option or price.

  • 4

    Mark it unresolved. When the detail is missing, note it and check again when it is available.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a LoongBuy spreadsheet?

A LoongBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links for use with agent shopping services. It is a pointer to browse and re-check, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official LoongBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by LoongBuy or Google.

What are the four levels of browse depth?

Overview gives direction and an entry point. Category narrows the product range without proving a specific option. Listing shows the visible clues at hand, which may still lack context. Current page is where you re-read the specific option and its details. Each level answers different questions.

Can a category label prove a specific option or price?

No. A category label narrows the range, but it cannot prove a specific option, price, or stock state. Those must be re-read on the current page, not carried over from a wider label.

What should I do when the detail disappears?

Stop. Do not fill the gap from a wider level. A missing detail is a boundary, not a reason to infer. Mark the point unresolved and re-check the current page when it is available.

Where do I confirm price, options, images, and shipping?

On the current page itself, at the moment you are reading it. Older descriptions, list thumbnails, and wider labels cannot stand in for the specific options and facts on the page you are actually viewing.
