About This Resource

A browse-depth method with optical crop frames, built around a shared LoongBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a LoongBuy spreadsheet. It offers four levels of browse depth — overview, category, listing, and current page — so each conclusion is matched to the level of detail it actually came from.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official LoongBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by LoongBuy or Google. It is not a store, a product database, a QC certification, a shopping agent, an order tracker, or a fee calculator.

The browse-depth method

Everything here follows four levels. Citron marks the overview. Lavender marks the category. Crimson marks the listing. Cobalt marks the current page. A wider label is only a frame — it never proves a specific option, price, or stock state.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any conclusion.
  4. Confirm the specifics on the current page you are viewing.

Resolution example — not product detail, image analysis, database depth, or verification.
